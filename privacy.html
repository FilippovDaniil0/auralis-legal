Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Moro ("Moro", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Moro mobile application.

Moro is an anxiety relief and reflection app. It is not a medical, mental health, diagnostic, or emergency service. If you need urgent help, contact local emergency services or a qualified professional.

Information You Provide

Depending on how you use the app, you may provide:

  • name and email address entered during onboarding;
  • onboarding answers and anxiety check-in responses;
  • mood ratings, daily logs, completed practices, streaks, and reflection history;
  • notification preferences;
  • subscription status and purchase information processed by the App Store, Google Play, and RevenueCat.

How We Use Information

We use information to personalize calming practices, daily reflections, cards, and insights; calculate streaks, averages, and mood statistics; send local reminders; manage premium access; and improve app reliability.

AI Processing

Moro may send a limited version of your recent mood history, anxiety check-in answers, completed practices, and current app context to our backend on Supabase. The backend may use DeepSeek to generate daily content and AI insights. We do not send your password to AI providers.

Local Storage

Some app data is stored locally on your device, including onboarding status, mood history, streaks, completed practices, and cached daily content. You can delete app data from the Profile screen by using "Delete account", or by deleting the app from your device.

Third-Party Services

Moro may use Supabase, DeepSeek, RevenueCat, Apple App Store, and Google Play. These services process data according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Payments

Payments are processed by Apple or Google. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, Moro may schedule local reminders on your device. You can disable notifications in the app or in your device settings.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@auralis.app.
