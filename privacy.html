Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Auralis ("Auralis", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Auralis mobile application.

Auralis is a wellness and reflection app. It is not a medical, mental health, diagnostic, or emergency service. If you need urgent help, contact local emergency services or a qualified professional.

Information You Provide

Depending on how you use the app, you may provide:

  • name and email address entered during onboarding;
  • zodiac sign and onboarding answers;
  • mood ratings, daily logs, streaks, and reflection history;
  • notification preferences;
  • subscription status and purchase information processed by the App Store, Google Play, and RevenueCat.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • personalize daily reflections, cards, and insights;
  • calculate streaks, medians, and mood statistics;
  • send local reminders if you enable notifications;
  • manage premium access and restore purchases;
  • improve app reliability and user experience.

AI Processing

Auralis may send a limited version of your recent mood history, onboarding answers, and current app context to our backend on Supabase. The backend may use DeepSeek to generate daily content and AI insights. We do not send your password to AI providers.

AI-generated content is for reflection and general wellness support only. It should not be treated as professional advice.

Local Storage

Some app data is stored locally on your device, including onboarding status, mood history, streaks, and cached daily content. You can delete local app data from the Profile screen by using "Delete local account", or by deleting the app from your device.

Third-Party Services

Auralis may use the following services:

  • Supabase for backend functions and content caching;
  • DeepSeek for AI-generated reflections and insights;
  • RevenueCat for subscription status and purchase management;
  • Apple App Store and Google Play for payment processing and subscription management.

These services process data according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Payments

Payments are processed by Apple or Google. We do not receive or store your full payment card details. Subscription status may be shared with RevenueCat so the app can unlock premium access and restore purchases.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, Auralis may schedule local reminders on your device. You can disable notifications in the app or in your device settings.

Data Retention

Local data remains on your device until you delete it, reset the app, or uninstall the app. Cached AI content may be stored by our backend to reduce repeated generation and improve performance.

Your Choices

  • You can disable notifications at any time.
  • You can manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple or Google account settings.
  • You can delete local app data from the Profile screen.
  • You can contact us to request help with privacy questions.

Children's Privacy

Auralis is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect app data. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@auralis.app.
